Garden Logic Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective September 26, 2026. Developer: Yingshuai Wang. Contact: liw58736@gmail.com.

Local play

Your progress, preferences, chosen nickname, avatar and tool inventory are stored on your device. We do not operate an account system or receive this local gameplay data on our servers. No advertising or analytics SDK is included, and the app does not track you across apps or websites.

Optional Apple services

If you choose iCloud backup, Apple stores your selected save data in your iCloud account. You can request deletion of that cloud copy from the profile screen. Game Center processes the scores you submit and your Game Center player identity. The guest daily league is local and uses simulated opponents. Apple processes in-app purchases; the app uses verified transaction identifiers to deliver tools and avoid duplicate grants. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them only to respond and troubleshoot. You may request deletion by email; we retain support correspondence only as needed for resolving the request and applicable obligations.

Your choices

No login is required for the core game. iCloud and Game Center are optional. Delete the app to remove its local data; delete the cloud backup separately before uninstalling if you used it. Apple handles purchase history and any refund requests.

联系我们时，我们会收到你主动提供的邮件地址、内容和附件，仅用于回复和排查问题，可邮件申请删除。卸载可删除本机数据；使用过云备份的用户应单独删除云端副本。购买记录、退款由 Apple 管理。

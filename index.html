Garden Logic

A colorful owl logic puzzle for iPhone and iPad. Explore 20 original puzzles, learn through an interactive tutorial, and personalize your owl.

Tap to mark a space; double-tap to place an owl. One owl per color region, row and column. Owls must not touch, even diagonally.

English and Simplified Chinese. Play without an account. Optional Game Center and iCloud features use your Apple account.

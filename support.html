How to play / 玩法

Tap once to exclude a square, double-tap to place an owl. Each row, column and color region contains exactly one owl. Owls cannot touch, including diagonally. Replay the tutorial in Settings.

单击排除，双击点亮；每行、每列、每个颜色区域各一只猫头鹰，猫头鹰不可相邻（含对角）。设置中可重看教学。

Purchases and saves / 购买与存档

Tool packs are optional one-time consumable purchases handled by Apple. Used tools cannot be restored. Keep the app installed to retain local items and progress. Optional iCloud backup is available from your profile; check the timestamp before replacing a local save.

道具包为可选的一次性消耗型内购，由 Apple 处理。已使用道具不能恢复。个人资料页面可手动备份及恢复 iCloud 存档，请核对时间后再覆盖。

Leaderboard / 排行榜

The guest daily league uses clearly labeled simulated opponents. Optional Game Center leaderboards require Apple Game Center sign-in.
